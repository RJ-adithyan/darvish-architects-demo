Dr. Vikrant Residence

Wada walls and coastal laterite in one home.

Dr. Vikrant Patkar's home in Mumbai blends North and South Indian influences. It joins the thermal comfort of Deccan wadas with monsoon-ready coastal features.

Type
Traditional residence
Location
Mumbai
Size
5,358 sq ft

Stone, laterite, carved wood.

Thick stone walls regulate temperature and last. Locally sourced laterite adds weather resistance. Carved wooden elements and wide verandas link old craft to modern comfort.

A teak-toned living room.

Dark teak furniture, patterned cushions and tall curtains set a warm, grounded mood in the main sitting room.
