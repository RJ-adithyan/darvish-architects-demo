Rashid Villa

A calm villa of marble, warm wood and soft light.

Rashid Villa is a contemporary home that balances the owner's lifestyle with modern architecture. A pool room, a large kitchen and wide marble corridors sit alongside the main rooms.

Comfort in emptiness.

The architects built the house around a soothing, uncluttered quality. Marble floors and contemporary lighting keep every room fresh and inviting.

The living room sets the tone.

An eye-catching waved ceiling and plenty of seating make the living room the hub of the house. Bedrooms use 3D wall boards and glossy marble outlines for a calm finish.
